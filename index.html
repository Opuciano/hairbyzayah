The Brand

Beauty Crafted With Passion

Hair by Zayah Beauty Hub is a registered Nigerian beauty and hair business based in Rivers State, dedicated to professional hair artistry and premium hair products. Every appointment is treated as a craft — precise, considered, and personal.

From intricate braiding to flawless wig installation, our approach blends technical skill with an eye for detail, so every client leaves not just styled, but transformed — carrying a little more confidence than they walked in with.
